Definition

The anchor (<a>) element creates a hyperlink. It can link to another page, a file, an email address, or a location within the same page.

  • href: target URL or path.
  • target: where to open the link (e.g. _self, _blank).
  • download: suggests downloading the linked resource.
  • rel: relationship between the current document and the linked resource (useful with target="_blank").
